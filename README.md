## Exercise Sequence

### 1. Retail Checkout Simulation
- File: exercise1_checkout.py
- Enters item prices until the user enters `0`, then reports the total, average price, and item count.

### 2. Market Survey Analyzer
- File: exercise2_survey.py
- Counts sample customer preferences and reports each product's percentage of all votes.

### 3. Expense Report Categorizer
- File: exercise3_expenses.py
- Totals expenses for each category and calculates the overall expense total.

### 4. Sales Commission Calculator
- File: exercise4_commissions.py
- Calculates each employee's 10% commission and prints a commission leaderboard.

### 5. Stock Portfolio Tracker
- File: exercise5_portfolio.py
- Calculates the portfolio's value and simulates a week of stock-price changes.

### 6. Bank Loan Repayment Simulator
- File: exercise6_loan_simulator.py
- Simulates monthly interest and payments, then reports how many months it takes to repay the loan.

### 7. Simple Supply Chain Tracker
- File: exercise7_supply_chain.py
- Adds inventory across warehouses and reports the total stock of each product.

### 8. Customer Loyalty Tiers
- File: exercise8_loyalty_tiers.py
- Assigns customers a Bronze, Silver, or Gold tier and counts the customers in each tier.

### 9. Business Growth Projection
- File: exercise9_growth_projection.py
- Projects revenue for ten years using the entered annual growth rate.

### 10. Startup Pitch Deck Visualizer
- File: exercise10_pitch_chart.py
- Displays projected revenue as a simple text bar chart made with `#` characters.

## Run an exercise

```bash
python3 exercise1_checkout.py
```
